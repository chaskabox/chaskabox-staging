OUR STORY

Bachpan ka zaiqa, with a personal touch.

ChaskaBox is a Pakistani online snack store built for the cravings people remember: school-canteen favourites, chatpata picks, chocolates, biscuits, imli, gums and curated Chaska Boxes.

What makes ChaskaBox different?

Instead of trying to feel like a giant supermarket, ChaskaBox focuses on a more personal experience. Orders are picked from the market, packed carefully, and sent across Pakistan.

Make Pakistani snack shopping simple, trustworthy and fun — while keeping the nostalgia that makes these products special.
