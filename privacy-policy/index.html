PRIVACY

How your information is used.

ChaskaBox collects information needed to provide shopping, checkout and account features. This page describes the website implementation currently used by ChaskaBox.

Information you may provide

  • Name, phone number, delivery address and city during checkout.
  • Email and account information if you choose to sign in.
  • Saved addresses, wishlist information and account profile details when account features are enabled.
  • Messages or evidence you send to support.

Browser storage

The website uses browser local storage for features such as cart contents, wishlist, recently viewed products, preferences and a limited non-sensitive recent-purchase summary used for reorder reminders. The checkout code is designed not to persist the customer's full name, phone number or delivery address in local storage after a successful order. Browser storage remains on the device unless it is cleared or synced through an account feature.

Service providers

The current site can use Supabase for account and order-history features, Google for optional sign-in, and FormSubmit for checkout email notifications while the central order backend is being upgraded. Those services may process information needed to provide their functions under their own terms and privacy practices.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions or requests relating to information held by ChaskaBox, contact Chaskabox.mzg@gmail.com or WhatsApp 0332-0005381.
